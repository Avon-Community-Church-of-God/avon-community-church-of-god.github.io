Good questions

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. Come as you are.

What happens when I arrive?

Stop by the welcome desk in the lobby and say hello. If you have kids, we'll check them in and a volunteer will walk you to their classroom.

What about my kids?

We have a nursery for babies, Kingdom Kids for toddlers through 5th grade, and Kingdom Teens for middle and high school. All meet during worship. Every child is checked in, classroom doors stay locked from the inside during the service, and only the parent who dropped off a child can pick them up. Learn more

Do I have to be a member?

No. We don't keep a membership roll. If you belong to Jesus, you belong here, and if you're still figuring that out, you're welcome too.

Anything during the week?

Yes. Bible study meets Wednesdays at 6:00 PM.
