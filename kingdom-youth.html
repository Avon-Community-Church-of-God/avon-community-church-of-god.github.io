Kingdom Youth

Growing up in God's family

Kingdom Youth is our ministry for kids and teens. Every Sunday during worship, your kids get to learn about Jesus in a way that fits their age, with leaders who care about them.

Kingdom KidsKingdom Teens

Kingdom Kids

Bible stories, songs, crafts and friends for toddlers through 5th grade. We also have a nursery for babies.

WhenSundays during worship
WhoToddlers through 5th grade

First time? Check in at the welcome desk in the lobby, and a volunteer will walk you to your child's classroom. Regular attenders can head straight to class.

Kingdom Teens

A place for middle and high schoolers to ask real questions, dig into the Bible, and build friendships that last.

WhenSundays during worship
WhoMiddle and high school
Suggested photo: teens around a table with Bibles open

Safe and cared for

Every child is checked in, and classroom doors are locked from the inside during the service. Only the parent who dropped off a child can pick them up.
